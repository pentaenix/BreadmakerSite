The light goes out
Mara had learned that a lighthouse could be quiet in a hundred different ways, but never this one.
The lamp had stopped turning. One by one, the lights of Bellweather vanished into the dark.
The telephone rang. Nobody called the lighthouse after midnight. Mara crossed the kitchen and answered.
“You need to leave,” a voice said. “Before the tide turns.”
She looked at the black windows. Far below, a single light moved across the water.
