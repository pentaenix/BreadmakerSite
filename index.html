Before you start

A few useful answers.

The book is the point. Here’s where Breadmaker fits around it.

What kind of writing is Breadmaker for?

Breadmaker is a writing app for long-form fiction. Draft chapters in Manuscript, collect ideas on Canvas, follow characters and plot threads with Maps, and keep revision work in Tasks. Use as much or as little of the workshop as your book needs.

Can I write in a browser?

Yes. Open the hosted web app and sign in with a Breadmaker account. The local editor runs on your own computer and does not require an account.

Do I need a subscription?

The local editor is free and open source. Premium plans and optional cloud services are in preparation. Pricing and availability will be published here before subscriptions are offered.

Can I take my project with me?

You can export and import complete Breadmaker projects. Checkpoints also let you save versions of a chapter or the whole manuscript as you revise. Your book belongs to you.
