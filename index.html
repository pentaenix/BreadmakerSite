Before you start

Frequently asked questions

A few practical details.

What kind of writing is Breadmaker for?

Novels, novellas, and other long-form fiction. Plan as much or as little as you like, then write, edit, and collect reader feedback in the same project.

Can I write in a browser?

Yes. Open the hosted web app and sign in. The local editor runs on your own computer and does not require an account.

Do I need a subscription?

The local editor is free and open source. Premium plans and optional cloud services are in preparation. Pricing and availability will be published here before subscriptions are offered.

Which languages does Breadmaker support?

The interface is available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese. Open Breadmaker → Language… to choose. This changes the interface, not the words in your manuscript. See the language guide.

Can I take my project with me?

The Export menu offers PDF and DOCX (.docx) for the manuscript, plus Breadmaker project files and Project JSON. Project JSON carries text and planning data without images or media. Your book belongs to you.
