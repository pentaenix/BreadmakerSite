A story workshop

Sit down.
Write.

Breadmaker is built to make every part of writing a book as simple as possible. Plan, outline, research, write, and edit without setting up a system first.

ManuscriptCanvasMapsTasks

Manuscript

Write the book.

Manuscript is where the book lives. Add chapters and scene headings, move through the draft from the sidebar, or hide the rest of the app and just write.

FocusSplit ViewMarkupEditor’s Eye
Live Manuscript interface

Canvas

Think somewhere that isn’t a page.

Canvas is the loose planning space. Put characters, scenes, research, questions, or whatever else you need on the board and move it around until it makes sense.

Live Canvas interface

Maps

See the story move.

Maps lets you track things across the book. Use a line for a character, plot, theme, or anything else you want to follow from chapter to chapter.

Live Maps interface

Tasks

Remember what the book needs.

Tasks is where you keep the work the book still needs. Add milestones when a fix has more than one step, finish it, and move on.

Live Tasks interface

Linked to the manuscript

Keep the work connected.

A Canvas node, Map station, or Task can point back to the part of the manuscript it belongs to. Open the link and go straight there.

Checkpoints

Save it. Keep going.

Checkpoints save the current version of a chapter or the entire manuscript so you can keep going. Compare it later, restore it, or export it when you need it.

Live Checkpoints interface

Editor’s Eye

Make familiar words feel unfamiliar again.

Editor’s Eye temporarily changes the look of your draft while you edit. Same words, different font, spacing, and page treatment, so familiar sentences are easier to see again.

Live Editor’s Eye interface

Yours

Your book belongs to you.

Breadmaker is local-first and open source. No account is required to write locally. No subscription is required to keep writing locally.
