Hosted account age requirements

Minimum age: 13, except the country thresholds listed below. This includes Mexico and the United States. Checked 8 November 2026.

14+

Austria, Bermuda, Chile, Colombia, Cyprus, Italy, Lithuania, North Macedonia, Peru, South Korea, Spain, Venezuela

15+

Czech Republic, Denmark, Egypt, France, Greece, Monaco, Serbia, Vietnam

16+

Albania, Andorra, Aruba, Bulgaria, Caribbean Netherlands, Croatia, Curaçao, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Poland, Romania, San Marino, Sint Maarten, Slovakia, Slovenia

Google account age requirements

This is an eligibility rule, not a statement that age is verified. Google sign-in does not establish eligibility or parental permission for Breadmaker. There is no supervised-child route in the hosted service at present. Local open-source use is separate.
