Before you sign in

Who can use Breadmaker Cloud today?

Breadmaker Cloud is currently open to approved alpha accounts. If you arrived here after signing in, that account may not have active alpha access. You can return using an approved alpha account.

Paid plans are not available yet. Access currently requires an approved alpha account.

What would I choose Cloud for?

Choose Cloud when you want to open your writing space in a browser and return to your Cloud projects from another computer. Choose the local editor when offline, accountless writing matters more to you.

Does Cloud automatically sync my local projects?

No. Use export and import to move a compatible project copy between editions. Changes do not sync automatically.

Can I take my writing with me?

You can export your manuscript as PDF or DOCX. Keep your own exported copies of important work while Breadmaker is in pre-alpha.
