A few practical details

What’s included in Breadmaker Cloud?

Your writing space in the browser: Manuscript for your draft, Canvas and Maps for planning, and Tasks and Checkpoints for revision. Sign in from another computer to return to your Cloud projects.

What does Cloud add?

Open Breadmaker without a local installation and access your Cloud projects from another computer. Your writing, planning, and revision tools are there with you.

Can I still use Breadmaker locally?

Yes. The local editor stays free, open source, and accountless. Offline writing and backups to your own folders, external drives, or connected backup services work in the local editor. Cloud needs an internet connection and does not provide those local backup connections.

Can I bring my local projects into Cloud?

Yes. Use export and import to move a compatible project copy between editions. Local and Cloud copies stay separate. Changes do not sync automatically.

Can I take my writing with me?

You can export your manuscript as PDF or DOCX. Keep your own exported copies of important work.
