What Breadmaker includes

The main tools.

ManuscriptWrite chapters and sections.
CanvasArrange notes and ideas spatially.
MapsFollow story lines across the manuscript.
TasksTrack writing and editing work.
LinksConnect planning and tasks to manuscript locations.
CheckpointsSave deliberate versions of a chapter or the whole manuscript.
Editor’s EyeTemporarily change how the manuscript looks while editing.
ReviewsGather feedback on a fixed version of your draft.
TargetsChoose optional word, character, or time goals.
LanguagesChoose from seven interface languages.
01

Project

Create a project.

On first launch, you can start writing immediately. Breadmaker creates a project automatically. To create another project:

  1. Click the Breadmaker button.
  2. Click New Project….
  3. Enter a project name.
  4. Click Create.
02

Manuscript

Add a chapter.

  1. Open Manuscript.
  2. Open ⋮ beside Full Text, then click Add Chapter.
  3. Rename the chapter.
  4. Write in the page.

The Manuscript toolbar also holds Notes, Markers, Images, Cuts, and Narration. Use the bookmark beside the page to return to your place. These stay beside the draft as you write and revise.

03

Canvas

Create a Canvas Card.

  1. Open Canvas.
  2. Create a Canvas.
  3. Click Add Card, then choose a Card.
  4. Place the Card, then type its title and notes.
04

Maps

Create a Map.

  1. Open Maps.
  2. Click + in the Maps sidebar.
  3. Open the Map’s ⋮ menu, then click New Line.
  4. Right-click the Line to add a Station or Span.
05

Tasks

Create a Task.

  1. Open Tasks.
  2. Click + Task.
  3. Enter the task.
  4. Click Create.
06

Links

Add a link.

  1. Open the details of a Task, Canvas Card, or Map item and click Add link.
  2. Choose what it should link to.
  3. Click Add link.
07

Checkpoints

Create a Checkpoint.

  1. Open Manuscript.
  2. Click the Checkpoints icon.
  3. Click + Checkpoint.
  4. Choose the chapter or entire book, then click Create checkpoint.
08

Editor’s Eye

Use Editor’s Eye.

  1. Open Manuscript.
  2. Click the eye icon.
  3. Click it again to return to the normal manuscript view.
  4. Turn it on again for a different font, color, spacing, and page layout. Your text stays the same.
09 · Written guide

Reviews

Set up a Review.

  1. Open Reviews, then click Create Review.
  2. Name it and choose the scope: the entire manuscript, selected Chapters, or selected Scenes.
  3. Choose permissions for reading, comments and highlights, suggestions, or feedback, then click Create.
  4. Use Participants and Questionnaires to prepare the round.
  5. Click Start Review…, read the confirmation, then Start Review.

Starting a round freezes what reviewers see and creates permanent Checkpoints. Later manuscript edits do not change that round’s snapshot.

10 · Written guide

Targets

Choose your own pace.

  1. Open the Breadmaker menu, then Targets….
  2. Turn on Enable targets and click + Add target.
  3. Choose a measure: words, characters, time in app, or active time.
  4. Choose Daily, Weekly, or Monthly, enter the target, and click Save.
  5. Open Targets again to adjust a goal, choose an optional chime, or view History….

What counts?

Words and characters measure net manuscript changes. Deleting text can reduce progress. Time in app counts foreground time; active time pauses after inactivity.

Targets starts off. Turning it off stops new tracking and keeps saved goals and history. Each project has its own goals.

11 · Written guide

Languages

Use the interface in your language.

  1. Open the Breadmaker menu.
  2. Choose Language….
  3. Search or select a language in the list.

This changes menus, labels, and messages. It does not translate your manuscript or select a narration voice.

Seven interface languages

  • English
  • Español Spanish
  • Français French
  • Italiano Italian
  • 日本語 Japanese
  • Português (Brasil) Brazilian Portuguese
  • 简体中文 Simplified Chinese
